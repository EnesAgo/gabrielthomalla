About

Hi, I am Gabriel.

I built two startups. raised $150M+.
Scaled teams from 5 to 100+.

What nearly broke me?

Too many people, too fast.
No time to think. No systems. Just motion.

We weren't scaling - we were reacting.
And I was stuck in the middle of everything.

At Kaia Health, I led growth.
At Alaiko, I built and rebuilt product, ops, finance, and CX.

While scaling teams as a founder, I had to unlearn being helpful.
And start designing things that worked without me.

Now I share the stuff I wish I had earlier:
What to fix. What to ignore. What breaks when you grow.

If you're scaling and everything feels harder - you're not alone.
